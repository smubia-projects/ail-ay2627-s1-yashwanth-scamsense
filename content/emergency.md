🚨 <b>If you think you are being scammed right now</b>

1. <b>Stop.</b> Don't send any more money, don't read out OTPs or passwords, and don't install anything they ask for.
2. <b>Hang up / stop replying.</b> Real police, banks and government agencies will never ask you to transfer money or keep a call secret.
3. <b>Call your bank</b> using the number on the back of your card or in its official app — not a number the caller gave you. Ask them to block transactions and secure your accounts.
4. <b>Call the ScamShield Helpline: 1799</b> if you're unsure whether it's a scam. They can also connect you to your bank.
5. <b>Report to the police:</b> call the Police Hotline 1800-255-0000 or report online at police.gov.sg/i-witness. In an emergency or if you're in danger, call 999.
6. <b>Keep evidence</b> — don't delete chats, call logs or screenshots.

You are not alone and it is not your fault. Scammers are professionals.
