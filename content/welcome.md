🛡️ <b>ScamSense</b> — a second pair of eyes for suspicious messages

Scammers rarely work alone: one "person" contacts you, hands you to a "police officer" or "bank manager", and a third voice backs the story up. Most checkers read one message at a time. <b>ScamSense reads the whole conversation</b>, works out who is doing what, and tells you what to do next.

<b>How to use it</b>
1. Tap <b>Check a conversation</b>.
2. <b>Forward</b> the messages to me (I keep each sender's name), or paste them like <code>Name: message</code> or a WhatsApp export.
3. Tap <b>Analyse</b>.

Your phone numbers, NRIC, card/bank numbers, emails and OTPs are removed <i>before</i> any AI sees the text, and nothing is stored. See /privacy.

<i>ScamSense is an aid, not a guarantee. It never says a message is "safe".</i>
