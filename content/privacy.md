🔒 <b>Privacy — what ScamSense does with your messages</b>

• <b>Redaction first.</b> Before any AI model sees your text, ScamSense replaces NRIC/FIN numbers, phone numbers, card numbers, bank account numbers, emails and OTP/PIN codes with placeholders like [PHONE_1]. Sender names are also replaced with "Sender A/B…" for the AI. Only this redacted text is ever sent to the AI provider.
• <b>Not perfect.</b> Redaction uses pattern matching. Names, addresses or other details written inside message text are <u>not</u> removed — please don't include more than you need.
• <b>No storage.</b> Messages are held in memory only while you are using the bot (they are dropped when you tap Analyse, or after about 30 minutes of inactivity). There is no database. Logs never contain message text.
• <b>Screenshots (if enabled).</b> A screenshot is read by OCR <u>on the bot's own server</u> and turned into text, which then goes through the same redaction. The image itself is never sent to the AI provider and is not stored. OCR can make mistakes. Voice is not supported.
• <b>Your AI provider.</b> The redacted text is processed by an AI provider (Groq) under their terms to produce the analysis.
• <b>Telegram.</b> Telegram itself keeps your chat with this bot according to its own policies. You can delete the chat in Telegram.
• <b>Fixed advice.</b> The emergency and recovery checklists are fixed text written in advance, not generated by AI.

<i>ScamSense is an aid, not a guarantee. When in doubt, call the ScamShield Helpline on 1799.</i>
