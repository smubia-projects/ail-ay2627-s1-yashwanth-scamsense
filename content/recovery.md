💸 <b>If you have already sent money or shared details</b>

Act fast — minutes matter.

1. <b>Call your bank immediately</b> (number on the back of your card / official app). Tell them it's a scam, ask them to block or recall the transfer and to freeze or secure your accounts and cards.
2. <b>Report to the police</b> right away: Police Hotline 1800-255-0000, or online at police.gov.sg/i-witness. Give them the details, account numbers and time of each transfer. For urgent help, call 999.
3. <b>Keep all evidence:</b> screenshots, chat history, phone numbers, account numbers, transaction receipts. Don't delete anything.
4. <b>If you shared passwords, OTPs or Singpass details:</b> change them now from a device you trust, and tell your bank. If you installed an app they sent, switch off the phone's internet, uninstall it and get help before banking again.
5. <b>Don't pay anyone who offers to "recover" your money</b> for a fee — that's often a second scam.
6. <b>Tell someone you trust.</b> Scammers rely on secrecy.

The ScamShield Helpline (1799) can also advise you and connect you to your bank, but it can't file a police report for you.
